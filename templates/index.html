Cloud & YouTube Cookie Setup

When deployed to cloud hosts (Vercel, Render, AWS), YouTube restricts datacenter IPs with "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot". Adding your YouTube cookies fixes this immediately.

1
Export YouTube Cookies:

Install the "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" Chrome/Firefox extension and export your YouTube cookies.

2
Add to Vercel (Permanent):

Go to Vercel Dashboard → Settings → Environment Variables → Add YOUTUBE_COOKIES with your cookie text, then redeploy.
